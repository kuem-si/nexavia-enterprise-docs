## What Each Tier Adds

<div class="tier-grid">
	<div class="tier-card tier-basic">
		<h3>Basic</h3>
		<ul>
			<li>Inventory and GIS location</li>
			<li>Current status and mains monitoring</li>
			<li>Basic electricity consumption data</li>
			<li>Active and inactive outlet overview</li>
			<li>30 days of history</li>
			<li>Manual fault reporting and basic email alerts</li>
			<li>Standard data export and business-hours support</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-advanced">
		<h3>Advanced</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Basic, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>Automated online/offline monitoring</li>
			<li>Outlet-level control</li>
			<li>Real-time electrical measurements</li>
			<li>Power/current limit violation detection</li>
			<li>Configurable thresholds and alerting rules</li>
			<li>Billing data preparation</li>
			<li>12 months of history</li>
			<li>Advanced dashboards, reports, and monthly usage report</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-pro">
		<h3>Pro</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Basic and Advanced, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>24/7 monitoring and alerting</li>
			<li>Power quality and capacity risk monitoring</li>
			<li>Unlimited data history</li>
			<li>Custom dashboards, reports, and user roles</li>
			<li>External integrations and API access</li>
			<li>Priority change requests and SLA response times</li>
			<li>Quarterly service review with optimisation recommendations</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
</div>

## Capability Matrix

<div class="tier-matrix">

| Capability group | Basic | Advanced | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory and status | Distribution box and connection point inventory, GIS location, current equipment status, mains availability, and outlet overview. | Basic visibility plus fault, maintenance, and configuration change history. | Custom user roles for operational access. |
| Monitoring | Basic consumption data and 30 days of measurement/event history. | Automated online/offline monitoring, real-time power, voltage, current, and total energy monitoring. | 24/7 monitoring, power quality, phase load, imbalance, capacity, utilisation, and operational risk monitoring. |
| Control | Outlet overview only. | Individual control of each connection point. | Integration-driven operational control. |
| Alerts and faults | Manual fault reporting and basic email alerts. | Configurable alerts for outages, overloads, missing data, limit violations, and supported breakers. | Abnormal consumption, peak load, and potential misuse detection. |
| Assignment and billing | Not included. | Outlet assignment to guest, vessel, pitch, berth, or reservation, plus electricity billing data preparation. | Reservation, payment, reception, or property management integrations. |
| Reporting and data | Standard data export and 30 days of history. | 12 months of history, advanced dashboards and reports, and monthly usage/performance report. | Unlimited history, custom dashboards and reports, and quarterly optimisation review. |
| Integrations and support | Business-hours support. | Standard operational support. | API access, third-party integrations, priority change requests, priority support, and SLA response times. |

</div>
