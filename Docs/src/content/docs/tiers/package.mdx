## Tier Summary

**Basic** provides the essential view of the electrical distribution box and its connection points. It includes inventory, GIS location display, current equipment status, mains power availability monitoring, basic electricity consumption data, active and inactive outlet overview, 30 days of basic measurement and event history, manual fault reporting, basic email alerts, standard data export, and business-hours support.

**Advanced** adds operational monitoring and connection point control. It includes automated online and offline monitoring, individual control of each connection point, real-time power, voltage, current, and total energy monitoring, limit violation detection, outage, overload, and missing-data alerts, breaker monitoring where hardware supports it, configurable thresholds, outlet assignment to operational entities such as guests or reservations, billing data preparation, 12 months of data history, fault and change history, advanced dashboards and reports, and monthly equipment usage and performance reporting.

**Pro** is intended for critical or highly integrated electrical infrastructure operations. It includes automated 24/7 monitoring and alerting, power quality monitoring, phase load and imbalance monitoring, abnormal consumption and misuse detection, capacity and utilisation risk monitoring, unlimited data history, custom dashboards, reports, and user roles, reservation, payment, reception, or property management integrations, API access, priority change requests, priority technical support, SLA-based response times, and quarterly service review with optimisation recommendations.
