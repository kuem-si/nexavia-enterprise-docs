<div class="tier-hero">
	<p>Lighting tiers define the level of asset management, map visibility, luminaire control, fault handling, monitoring, analytics, and support included for lighting operations.</p>
</div>

<div class="tier-grid">
	<div class="tier-card tier-basic">
		<h2>Basic</h2>
		<p><strong>Choose this when:</strong></p>
		<p>You need lighting inventory, GIS visibility, preset control, manual fault reporting, and standard reporting.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-advanced">
		<h2>Advanced</h2>
		<p><strong>Choose this when:</strong></p>
		<p>You need automated device monitoring, automatic fault reporting, alarm handling, work orders, and energy analytics.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-pro">
		<h2>Pro</h2>
		<p><strong>Choose this when:</strong></p>
		<p>You need adaptive lighting, advanced dimming, multi-utility asset management, advanced reports, and enhanced SLA coverage.</p>
	</div>
</div>

## What Each Tier Adds

<div class="tier-grid">
	<div class="tier-card tier-basic">
		<h3>Basic</h3>
		<ul>
			<li>Asset inventory and GIS map</li>
			<li>Asset history</li>
			<li>Preset luminaire control</li>
			<li>Manual fault reporting</li>
			<li>Standard reports</li>
			<li>Standard support</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-advanced">
		<h3>Advanced</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Basic, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>Automatic fault reporting</li>
			<li>Automated device monitoring</li>
			<li>Alarm management</li>
			<li>Work-order management</li>
			<li>Energy consumption analytics</li>
			<li>Individual luminaire control</li>
			<li>Standard dimming profiles</li>
			<li>Optional SLA</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-pro">
		<h3>Pro</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Basic and Advanced, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>Advanced reports</li>
			<li>Advanced dimming profiles</li>
			<li>Adaptive or sensor-driven lighting</li>
			<li>Multi-utility asset management</li>
			<li>Enhanced SLA</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
</div>

<div class="tier-highlight">
	<p><strong>Key difference:</strong> Basic focuses on inventory and preset control, Advanced adds automated operations, and Pro adds adaptive lighting plus broader asset management.</p>
</div>

## Capability Matrix

<div class="tier-matrix">

| Capability group | Basic | Advanced | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Asset management | Lighting asset inventory, GIS map, and asset history. | Basic asset management plus operational workflows. | Multi-utility asset management. |
| Monitoring and faults | Manual fault reporting. | Automatic fault reporting and automated device monitoring. | Advanced capabilities with enhanced SLA coverage. |
| Alarms and work orders | Not included. | Alarm management and work-order management. | Advanced capabilities. |
| Control | Preset luminaire control. | Individual luminaire control and standard dimming profiles. | Advanced dimming profiles and adaptive or sensor-driven lighting. |
| Analytics and reports | Standard reports. | Energy consumption analytics and standard reports. | Advanced reports. |
| Support and SLA | Standard support. | Standard support with optional SLA. | Enhanced SLA. |

</div>
