## Tier Summary

**Basic** covers essential lighting visibility and control. It includes lighting asset inventory, GIS map and asset history, preset luminaire control, manual fault reporting, standard reports, and standard support.

**Advanced** adds automated operations. It includes Lighting Basic capabilities plus automatic fault reporting, automated device monitoring, alarm management, work-order management, energy consumption analytics, individual luminaire control, standard dimming profiles, and optional SLA.

**Pro** provides the most complete lighting operations package. It includes Lighting Advanced capabilities plus advanced reports, advanced dimming profiles, adaptive or sensor-driven lighting, multi-utility asset management, and enhanced SLA.
