## What Each Tier Adds

<div class="tier-grid">
	<div class="tier-card tier-basic">
		<h3>Monitored</h3>
		<ul>
			<li>Automated gateway online/offline monitoring</li>
			<li>Sensor heartbeat and missing-data alarms</li>
			<li>Email alerts</li>
			<li>Business-hours support</li>
			<li>Basic monthly availability report</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-advanced">
		<h3>Managed</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Monitored, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>24/7 automated monitoring and alerting</li>
			<li>Gateway VPN access and control</li>
			<li>Battery, RSSI, SNR, packet-loss, and abnormal-traffic monitoring</li>
			<li>Remote gateway diagnostics and restart</li>
			<li>Network Dashboard Insight and GIS View and Management</li>
			<li>Alert tuning, escalation contacts, and business-hours engineer response</li>
			<li>Monthly service report and four business hour acknowledgement target</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="tier-card tier-critical">
		<h3>Critical</h3>
		<p><strong>Everything in Monitored and Managed, plus:</strong></p>
		<ul>
			<li>24/7 human escalation</li>
			<li>Remote remediation</li>
			<li>Incident ownership and customer updates</li>
			<li>Defined SLA, for example 15 to 30 minute acknowledgement</li>
			<li>Root-cause analysis for major incidents</li>
			<li>Capacity and coverage-risk reviews</li>
			<li>Priority change requests and quarterly service review</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
</div>

## Capability Matrix

<div class="tier-matrix">

| Capability group | Monitored | Managed | Critical |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monitoring | Automated gateway online/offline monitoring and sensor heartbeat/missing-data alarms. | 24/7 automated monitoring plus battery, RSSI, SNR, packet-loss, and abnormal-traffic monitoring. | Managed monitoring plus 24/7 human escalation. |
| Alerts and escalation | Email alerts. | Alert tuning and escalation contacts. | Defined SLA and human escalation path. |
| Access and remediation | Not included. | Gateway VPN access and control, remote diagnostics, and restart. | Remote remediation and incident ownership. |
| Operational views | Basic availability visibility through reports. | Network Dashboard Insight plus GIS View and Management. | Capacity and coverage-risk reviews. |
| Response | Business-hours support. | Business-hours engineer response and four business hour acknowledgement target. | SLA acknowledgement, for example 15 to 30 minutes. |
| Reporting and governance | Basic monthly availability report. | Monthly service report. | Customer updates, root-cause analysis, priority change requests, and quarterly service review. |

</div>
