## Tier Summary

**Monitored** is intended for automated visibility and basic alerting. It covers gateway online or offline status, sensor heartbeat checks, missing-data alarms, email alerts, business-hours support, and a basic monthly availability report.

**Managed** adds active operational control and deeper network diagnostics. It includes everything in Monitored, plus 24/7 automated monitoring and alerting, gateway VPN access and control, remote diagnostics and restart, dashboard insight, GIS view and management, tuned alerting, escalation contacts, business-hours engineer response, monthly service reporting, and a four business hour acknowledgement target.

**Critical** is intended for environments where incidents require direct ownership and faster escalation. It includes everything in Managed, plus 24/7 human escalation, remote remediation, incident ownership, customer updates, a defined SLA such as 15 to 30 minute acknowledgement, root-cause analysis for major incidents, capacity and coverage-risk reviews, priority change requests, and quarterly service review.
